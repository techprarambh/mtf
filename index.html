Our Purpose

Creating opportunities where local talent meets local markets.

We believe that SHGs can become strong community enterprises when they receive the right guidance, market connections and practical support. Our approach is simple: understand the group, strengthen the business, improve the product and connect it to the right customer.

About Us

Building stronger communities through collective enterprise.

Maa Tripreswari Foundation is a community-focused NGO working to support Self Help Groups, women-led initiatives and grassroots entrepreneurship.

We focus on practical support rather than one-time assistance. Our goal is to help groups improve their planning, production, presentation, sales and long-term sustainability.
